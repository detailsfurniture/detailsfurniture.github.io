Submissions

Send us your project

It is free, and we would love to see your work. We look at what arrives and share the pieces that fit our curation. Payment never influences that choice. We cannot promise a reply or a publication date, but we appreciate every project you send.

Images
5 to 10 good photos, at least 2,000 px on the long side. Show the piece on its own and in a setting.
Credits
Who designed it, the brand or studio, who photographed it, and the year.
Text
Up to 100 words on the idea, the materials and how it is made.
Specs
Dimensions, materials and finishes.
Rights
A quick confirmation that the images are yours or you have permission, and that we can publish them with credit.
Links
Your website or press page, and your Instagram.
Send your project submissions@detailsfurniture.com
